About

I'm a Computer Science undergraduate at Air University, Islamabad, and I've been shipping full-stack projects since my first year.

Most of my work sits in the MERN and TypeScript ecosystems: React and Redux Toolkit on the front, Node, Express and FastAPI behind them, MongoDB and Firebase underneath. On mobile I work in React Native with Expo and in Flutter.

The project I'm proudest of is Radiant AI, a clinical decision-support tool that runs a YOLOv7-P6 detector entirely on-device to flag pediatric wrist fractures. It taught me how much care an interface needs when the person reading it is a clinician making a call.

Away from the editor I lead The Alphas Society at Air University, where I run wellbeing programming and handle logistics for university-scale events.
